La Salle Rambot Graphic

Overview

A custom mascot illustration for La Salle Institute, reimagining their ram mascot as a friendly robot character. The design combines school pride with a modern, approachable aesthetic suitable for athletics, merchandise, and digital use.

What I Did

Created the full character design—from concept sketches to final vector artwork—in Adobe Illustrator. The rambot features expressive eyes, bold school colors, and clean linework that scales well from small icons to large printed formats.
