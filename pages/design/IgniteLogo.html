Ignite Logo

Overview

A brand identity project creating a logo for "Ignite"—a concept that evokes energy, spark, and activation. The mark needed to be memorable, versatile across light and dark backgrounds, and scalable for both print and digital applications.

What I Did

Explored multiple visual directions—from flame motifs to abstract sparks—before refining the final concept in Adobe Illustrator. The logo uses strong typography and a dynamic visual element to capture the idea of ignition and forward momentum.
