News Website

Overview

A UX-focused redesign of the RIT University News website, created in Adobe XD for New Media Design Elements in the fall of 2018. The goal was to improve information hierarchy, navigation, and visual clarity so students and visitors could find news more easily.

What I Did

Conducted a full redesign process: wireframing, iterative mockups, and interactive prototypes in Adobe XD. Emphasized clear typography, consistent spacing, and intuitive navigation patterns. The final design maintains brand alignment while improving usability and reducing visual noise.
